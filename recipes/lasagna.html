Classic Lasagna

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups mozzarella
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • Spices (garlic, basil, oregano, salt, pepper)

Instructions

  1. Cook noodles according to package directions.
  2. Brown beef in a skillet, add tomato sauce and spices.
  3. Layer noodles, ricotta, meat sauce, and mozzarella in a baking dish.
  4. Repeat layers, finish with mozzarella on top.
  5. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 45 minutes. Let cool and serve.
