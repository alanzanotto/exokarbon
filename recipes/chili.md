## Ingredients
- 1 or 2 Sweet Onions
- Celery
- Carrots
- Ground beef (1kg)
- Crushed Tomatoes
- Diced Tomatos
- Kidney Beans 2 Cans
- Black Beans
- Corn
- Chili powder (3tablespoons)
- Oregeno (1 tablespoon)

## Instructions
1. Brown the beef
2. Add ingredients
3. Simmer for 30 minutes
